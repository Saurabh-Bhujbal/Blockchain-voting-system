How Elections Work

The Electoral Process

A structured, constitutional process overseen by the Election Commission to guarantee democratic integrity.

1

Electoral Roll Management

Continuous revision ensures all eligible citizens are accurately enrolled and no ineligible persons appear on voter lists.

2

Schedule Announcement

ECI announces election dates triggering the Model Code of Conduct - ensuring a level playing field for all parties.

3

Nomination & Campaigning

Candidates file nominations and campaign freely under MCC guidelines. Campaigning closes 48 hours before polling.

4

Polling & Declaration

Secure voting followed by transparent counting under returning officer supervision. Results declared after full audit.

Election Guidelines

Model Code of Conduct

The MCC is a constitutional cornerstone that governs all stakeholders during elections to ensure fairness, transparency and equality.

General Conduct

All parties and candidates must avoid activities aggravating communal or linguistic tensions. Criticism must be confined to policies and past records - never personal lives.

Meetings & Processions

Prior permission from local authorities is required for all public meetings and processions. Organizers must ensure law, order, and smooth traffic management.

Government Restrictions

The ruling party cannot announce new schemes, make ad-hoc appointments, or use government machinery for campaign advantage once the MCC comes into force.

Enforcement & Observers

ECI deploys General and Expenditure Observers across constituencies. Violations of specific offenses are punishable under the Penal Code and the Representation of the People Act.

No Religious Misuse

No party shall use places of worship for political propaganda. Election campaigning based on religion, caste, or community is strictly prohibited.

Election Expenditure

Strict limits apply to campaign expenditure. All expenses must be maintained in an account, reported to the ECI, and are subject to audit by designated observers.
