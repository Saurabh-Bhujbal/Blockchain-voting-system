About the Election Commission

The Election Commission is an autonomous constitutional authority responsible for administering election processes at the national, state and district level. The body administers elections to the National Parliament, State Legislative Assemblies, and the offices of the President and Vice President.

Our new decentralized voting system utilizes Ethereum blockchain technology coupled with DeepFace biometric AI to ensure 100% transparency, immutability, and security for every single vote cast in the digital era. This modernization effort removes geographical and physical barriers, empowering every legitimate citizen to participate in the democratic process seamlessly.

Our Mission & Vision

To maintain independence, integrity and autonomy in election administration, ensuring free, fair, and transparent elections with maximum citizen participation.
